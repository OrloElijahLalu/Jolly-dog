WHO WE ARE

Kanto & Co. is a neighborhood comfort-food kitchen built around two simple ideas: satisfying, home-style dishes and zero-hassle ordering. Inspired by classic Filipino favorites and everyday street-side staples, we serve generous, freshly prepared meals without the long wait.

With our online guest ordering system, there are no accounts to register and no apps to download. Simply pick your favorites from Our Menu, choose your pickup window, and collect your order fresh at our counter.

POLICIES

Disclaimer

All food and beverage images displayed on the Kanto & Co. website are for visual presentation purposes only; actual serving sizes and plating may vary slightly. Menu prices are listed in Philippine Pesos (₱) and are subject to change without prior notice. While we take care in food preparation, our kitchen handles common allergens including soy, wheat/gluten, dairy, eggs, peanuts, and seafood.

Terms and Conditions

  • In-Store Pickup Only: Kanto & Co. operates strictly on a self-pickup basis. We do not offer delivery services.
  • Guest Ordering & Verification: Customers must provide a valid name, phone number, and email address during checkout. A unique Order Reference Number is issued upon confirmation and must be presented at the counter to claim your order.
  • Payment Method: All online orders are settled via Cash on Pick-Up directly at the store counter.
  • Preparation & Unclaimed Orders: Kitchen preparation begins once your order is confirmed. To maintain food freshness and safety, orders left unclaimed for more than 45 minutes past the selected pickup time may be canceled.
  • Order Changes & Cancellations: Because meals are prepared fresh upon submission, orders cannot be modified or canceled once confirmed.

Privacy Policy

  • Information We Collect: Kanto & Co. uses a guest checkout process and does not require user accounts or passwords. We only collect your name, phone number, and email address at the time of checkout.
  • How We Use Your Information: Your contact details are used strictly to identify your order at the pickup counter, send your order confirmation receipt, and notify you regarding your pickup status.
  • Data Security & Sharing: We respect your privacy. Kanto & Co. does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third-party marketers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do you offer delivery?

No. Kanto & Co. is strictly in-store pickup only.

Do I need to create an account to place an order?

No. All orders use our guest checkout—just enter your name, phone number, and email address to confirm your order.

How do I pay for my order?

We accept Cash on Pick-Up. Simply present your Order Reference Number at the cashier counter when you arrive.

How soon will my food be ready?

ASAP orders are typically ready within 15 to 20 minutes. You can also schedule your pickup for 30 minutes or 1 hour ahead during checkout.

What if I forgot or lost my Order Reference Number?

You can check the confirmation sent to your email address, or give our counter staff the name and phone number you entered during checkout.
